# Slide 6 — The Most Striking Cognition Study

### Short-form video and cognition
**Nguyen et al., 2025 — Psychological Bulletin**
<span class="text-xs font-mono text-indigo-300 ml-2">(71 studies • 98,299 participants)</span>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-2">
  <div class="space-y-1.5 p-3.5 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10 text-xs">
    <div class="font-bold text-slate-300 uppercase tracking-wider">Observed Associations (Effect Sizes):</div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Overall cognition:</span> <b class="font-mono text-indigo-300">r = −.34</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Attention focus:</span> <b class="font-mono text-cyan-300">r = −.38</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Inhibitory control:</span> <b class="font-mono text-rose-300 font-bold">r = −.41</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Perceived stress:</span> <b class="font-mono text-amber-300">r ≈ .34</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between py-1"><span>Anxiety:</span> <b class="font-mono text-amber-300">r ≈ .33</b></div>
  </div>

  <div class="space-y-3 p-3.5 bg-indigo-950/60 rounded-xl border border-indigo-500/30 flex flex-col justify-center text-xs">
    <div class="font-extrabold text-sm text-white">The Strongest Signal:</div>
    <div class="text-slate-200">
      It is <b>NOT</b> “lower general IQ”. It is specifically <b>attention and inhibitory control</b>.
    </div>
    <div class="p-2.5 bg-white/5 rounded-lg border border-white/10 text-[11px] text-slate-300">
      <b>Inhibitory control:</b> The neurological ability to resist immediate impulses, suppress distractions, and sustain goal-directed focus.
    </div>
    <div class="text-[10px] text-slate-400 uppercase font-mono">
      ⚠️ Association, not proof of permanent causation.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

# Slide 7 — Mental Health and Sleep

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-3 my-2 text-xs">
  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="font-bold text-indigo-300">Ahmed et al., 2024</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] text-slate-400 font-mono">182 studies • 1,169,396 people</div>
    <p class="mt-2 text-slate-200">Problematic use strongly associated with:</p>
    <ul class="mt-1 space-y-0.5 text-slate-300">
      <li>• Depression & anxiety</li>
      <li>• Sleep disruption</li>
      <li>• Lower general wellbeing</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="font-bold text-purple-300">Du et al., 2024</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] text-slate-400 font-mono">209 studies • 252,337 people</div>
    <p class="mt-2 text-slate-200">Problematic network use correlations:</p>
    <ul class="mt-1 space-y-0.5 text-slate-300">
      <li>• Generalized anxiety: <b>r ≈ .39</b></li>
      <li>• Social anxiety: <b>r ≈ .44</b></li>
      <li>• Fear of Missing Out (FoMO): <b>r ≈ .50</b></li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="font-bold text-cyan-300">2025 Sleep Umbrella Review</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] text-slate-400 font-mono">867,003 adolescents</div>
    <p class="mt-2 text-slate-200">Consistent clinical triad:</p>
    <ul class="mt-1 space-y-0.5 text-slate-300">
      <li>• Later bedtimes</li>
      <li>• Shorter sleep duration</li>
      <li>• Significantly poorer sleep quality</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="p-3 bg-gradient-to-r from-indigo-900/60 to-purple-900/60 border border-indigo-400/30 rounded-xl text-center text-xs text-white font-semibold">
🎯 Main Takeaway: <b>Problematic, compulsive use</b> appears far more important than raw passive “screen time” alone.
</div>

# Slide 8 — But Does Using Less Actually Help?

<div class="text-xs uppercase font-bold tracking-widest text-emerald-400 mb-1">
Causal Evidence from Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs)
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 text-xs">
  <div class="p-3.5 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/30 space-y-2">
    <div class="font-bold text-emerald-300 text-sm">Castelo et al., 2025 — RCT</div>
    <div class="text-slate-300">Mobile internet completely blocked on smartphones for <b>2 weeks</b>.</div>
    <div class="p-2.5 bg-emerald-950/40 rounded-lg border border-emerald-500/20 text-emerald-200 font-medium">
      Measurable Results:
      <ul class="list-disc pl-4 mt-1 space-y-0.5">
        <li>Significantly better sustained attention</li>
        <li>Better mental health markers</li>
        <li>Elevated daily subjective wellbeing</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="font-bold text-white text-xs">
      🏆 <b>91% of participants</b> improved on at least one outcome.
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3.5 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10 space-y-2">
    <div class="font-bold text-indigo-300 text-sm">May et al., 2025 Meta-Analysis</div>
    <div class="text-slate-300">Pooled <b>10 randomized controlled trials</b> (N = 1,491 participants).</div>
    <p class="text-slate-200">
      Reducing or eliminating social media produced a <b>statistically significant reduction in depressive symptoms</b>.
    </p>
    <div class="p-2.5 bg-white/5 rounded-lg border border-white/10 text-[11px] text-slate-400">
      <b>Key caveat:</b> The mobile-internet RCT restricted overall mobile browsing, not solely social apps. Still, causal impact of disconnection is established.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

# Slide 10 — What the Evidence Does NOT Show

<div class="text-xs text-rose-400 font-bold uppercase tracking-wider mb-2">
Debunking Exaggerated Claims & Moral Panics
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-3 text-xs">
  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="text-rose-300 font-bold text-xs">❌ “Lowers General IQ”</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] font-bold text-slate-300 mt-1">Not established.</div>
    <p class="mt-1.5 text-slate-300 text-[11px]">
      <b>Sauce et al., 2022 (9,855 children):</b> Social-media use did <i>not</i> predict later decline in intelligence measures.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="text-rose-300 font-bold text-xs">❌ “Causes Structural Brain Damage”</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] font-bold text-slate-300 mt-1">Not established.</div>
    <p class="mt-1.5 text-slate-300 text-[11px]">
      <b>Nivins et al., 2024:</b> 4-year longitudinal neuroimaging (MRI) data did not support claims of cortical/striatal tissue atrophy.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="text-rose-300 font-bold text-xs">❌ “Quitting Magically Fixes All Happiness”</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] font-bold text-slate-300 mt-1">Not established.</div>
    <p class="mt-1.5 text-slate-300 text-[11px]">
      <b>Lemahieu et al., 2025:</b> Temporary total abstinence does not reliably improve general positive affect for all users.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-3 bg-white/10 border-l-4 border-indigo-400 rounded-r-xl text-xs text-slate-200">
💡 <b>Big Conclusion:</b> The scientific argument is for <b>substantially less and more intentional use</b> — not exaggerated claims of biological brain destruction.
</div>

# Slide 14 — Make It Specific (IF-THEN Planning)

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 text-xs my-2">
  <div class="p-3 bg-rose-950/40 border border-rose-500/30 rounded-xl">
    <span class="text-rose-300 font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-[10px]">Vague & Fails:</span>
    <div class="font-mono text-xs text-slate-300 mt-1">“I’ll try to use my phone less this week.”</div>
  </div>
  <div class="p-3 bg-emerald-950/40 border border-emerald-500/30 rounded-xl">
    <span class="text-emerald-300 font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-[10px]">Implementation Intention (Gollwitzer):</span>
    <div class="font-bold text-xs text-emerald-200 mt-1">IF [Trigger], THEN I will [Pre-decided Action].</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-2 text-xs mb-2">
  <div class="p-2 bg-white/5 rounded border border-white/10 text-[11px] text-slate-300">
    <b>Example 1:</b> <i>IF</i> I reach for Instagram while studying, <i>THEN</i> I put phone in another room until break.
  </div>
  <div class="p-2 bg-white/5 rounded border border-white/10 text-[11px] text-slate-300">
    <b>Example 2:</b> <i>IF</i> I get into bed, <i>THEN</i> my phone stays plugged in across the room.
