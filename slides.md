# Slide 6 — The Most Striking Cognition Study

### Short-form video and cognition
**Nguyen et al., 2025 — Psychological Bulletin**
<span class="text-xs font-mono text-indigo-300 ml-2">(71 studies, 98,299 participants)</span>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-2">
  <div class="space-y-1.5 p-3.5 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10 text-xs font-mono">
    <div class="font-bold text-slate-300 uppercase tracking-wider">Observed Effect Sizes (r):</div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Overall Cognition:</span> <b class="text-indigo-300">r = -0.34</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Sustained Attention:</span> <b class="text-cyan-300">r = -0.38</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Inhibitory Control:</span> <b class="text-rose-300 font-bold">r = -0.41</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between border-b border-white/5 py-1"><span>Perceived Stress:</span> <b class="text-amber-300">r ≈ 0.34</b></div>
    <div class="flex justify-between py-1"><span>Anxiety:</span> <b class="text-amber-300">r ≈ 0.33</b></div>
  </div>

  <div class="space-y-3 p-3.5 bg-indigo-950/60 rounded-xl border border-indigo-500/30 flex flex-col justify-center text-xs">
    <div class="font-extrabold text-sm text-white">The Dominant Empirical Signal:</div>
    <div class="text-slate-200">
      The primary finding is <b>NOT</b> generalized decline in intelligence. It is specifically <b>attention and inhibitory control</b>.
    </div>
    <div class="p-2.5 bg-white/5 rounded-lg border border-white/10 text-[11px] text-slate-300">
      <b>Inhibitory control:</b> The neurological capacity to resist immediate impulses, filter peripheral distractions, and maintain goal-directed focus.
    </div>
    <div class="text-[10px] text-slate-400 uppercase font-mono">
      Statistical association; does not establish permanent structural causation.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

# Slide 7 — Mental Health and Sleep

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-3 my-2 text-xs">
  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="font-bold text-indigo-300">Ahmed et al., 2024</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] text-slate-400 font-mono">182 studies | 1,169,396 subjects</div>
    <p class="mt-2 text-slate-200">Problematic use associated with:</p>
    <ul class="mt-1 space-y-0.5 text-slate-300">
      <li>- Elevated depressive symptoms</li>
      <li>- Anxiety and distress</li>
      <li>- Reduced general wellbeing</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="font-bold text-purple-300">Du et al., 2024</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] text-slate-400 font-mono">209 studies | 252,337 subjects</div>
    <p class="mt-2 text-slate-200">Problematic network use correlations:</p>
    <ul class="mt-1 space-y-0.5 text-slate-300 font-mono">
      <li>- Generalized anxiety: <b>r ≈ 0.39</b></li>
      <li>- Social anxiety: <b>r ≈ 0.44</b></li>
      <li>- Fear of Missing Out: <b>r ≈ 0.50</b></li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="font-bold text-cyan-300">2025 Sleep Umbrella Review</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] text-slate-400 font-mono">867,003 adolescents</div>
    <p class="mt-2 text-slate-200">Observed physiological disruption:</p>
    <ul class="mt-1 space-y-0.5 text-slate-300">
      <li>- Delayed sleep onset</li>
      <li>- Decreased overall sleep duration</li>
      <li>- Diminished subjective sleep quality</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="p-3 bg-indigo-950/70 border border-indigo-400/30 rounded-xl text-center text-xs text-white font-semibold">
Conclusion: <b>Problematic, compulsive usage patterns</b> carry significantly higher clinical relevance than aggregate passive screen hours alone.
</div>

# Slide 8 — But Does Using Less Actually Help?

<div class="text-xs uppercase font-mono font-bold tracking-widest text-emerald-400 mb-1">
Causal Findings from Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs)
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 text-xs">
  <div class="p-3.5 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/30 space-y-2">
    <div class="font-bold text-emerald-300 text-sm">Castelo et al., 2025 — RCT</div>
    <div class="text-slate-300">Smartphone mobile internet access blocked for <b>2 weeks</b>.</div>
    <div class="p-2.5 bg-emerald-950/40 rounded-lg border border-emerald-500/20 text-emerald-200 font-medium">
      Empirical Outcomes:
      <ul class="list-disc pl-4 mt-1 space-y-0.5">
        <li>Statistically significant increase in sustained attention</li>
        <li>Improved mental health markers</li>
        <li>Higher self-reported daily wellbeing</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="font-bold text-white text-xs font-mono">
      Result: 91% of participants demonstrated measurable improvement in at least one outcome.
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3.5 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10 space-y-2">
    <div class="font-bold text-indigo-300 text-sm">May et al., 2025 Meta-Analysis</div>
    <div class="text-slate-300">Synthesized <b>10 randomized controlled trials</b> (N = 1,491 participants).</div>
    <p class="text-slate-200">
      Restricting or removing social media produced a <b>statistically significant reduction in depressive symptoms</b>.
    </p>
    <div class="p-2.5 bg-white/5 rounded-lg border border-white/10 text-[11px] text-slate-400">
      <b>Methodological caveat:</b> The mobile internet intervention restricted general mobile browsing, not exclusively social applications.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

# Slide 10 — What the Evidence Does NOT Show

<div class="text-xs text-rose-400 font-mono font-bold uppercase tracking-wider mb-2">
Critical Review of Unsupported Claims
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-3 gap-3 text-xs">
  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="text-rose-300 font-bold text-xs">Claim: "Lowers General Intelligence"</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] font-bold text-slate-300 mt-1 font-mono">Not supported by evidence.</div>
    <p class="mt-1.5 text-slate-300 text-[11px]">
      <b>Sauce et al., 2022 (9,855 children):</b> Social media engagement did not predict longitudinal decline in measured cognitive ability.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="text-rose-300 font-bold text-xs">Claim: "Causes Structural Brain Damage"</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] font-bold text-slate-300 mt-1 font-mono">Not supported by evidence.</div>
    <p class="mt-1.5 text-slate-300 text-[11px]">
      <b>Nivins et al., 2024:</b> 4-year longitudinal neuroimaging (MRI) data did not support claims of cortical or striatal tissue atrophy.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 bg-slate-900/80 rounded-xl border border-white/10">
    <div class="text-rose-300 font-bold text-xs">Claim: "Total Abstinence Solves All Affect"</div>
    <div class="text-[11px] font-bold text-slate-300 mt-1 font-mono">Not supported by evidence.</div>
    <p class="mt-1.5 text-slate-300 text-[11px]">
      <b>Lemahieu et al., 2025:</b> Complete temporary abstinence does not reliably produce positive affect across all user demographics.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="mt-3 p-3 bg-white/10 border-l-4 border-indigo-400 rounded-r-xl text-xs text-slate-200">
<b>Summary:</b> The scientific basis supports <b>substantial reduction and deliberate friction</b> - rather than sensationalized physiological narratives.
</div>
